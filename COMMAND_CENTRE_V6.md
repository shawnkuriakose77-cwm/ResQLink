# RESQLINK COMMAND CENTRE V6

Command-side upgrade included in this build:

1. Command Centre master control room with clickable KPIs, live operational feed and system health.
2. National Map with Leaflet/OSM, India/state/district drill-down, incident points, hazard overlays and search.
3. Incidents with dynamic priority, status lifecycle, evidence and audit-aware priority changes.
4. Requests with 64 records, full lifecycle controls, filters, search and real CSV export of the current filtered dataset.
5. Responders roster with operational status, skills, location, vehicle, shift and assignment action.
6. Shelters with occupancy/capacity/service data and Google Maps directions.
7. Hospitals with beds, ICU, oxygen, blood, medicines, ambulance capacity, directions and call action.
8. Command Q&A with preset verified answers and Aarav Menon escalation contact.
9. Simulation laboratory with Flood/Cyclone/Heat/Landslide/Fire scenarios and animated scenario progression.
10. Broadcast builder with preview/confirm flow, active/scheduled/expired history and configured SOS-style alert tone.
11. Recovery workstreams with progress and operational cost/resource tracking.
12. Audit Trail with filters and real CSV export.
13. Missing Persons with search, cases, zones, sightings, teams and reunification workflow.
14. Verification with evidence checklist and functional review decisions.
15. Resources & Logistics lifecycle and dispatch actions.
16. Alerts & Escalations acknowledgement/ownership actions.
17. Intelligence & Analytics with date ranges, KPIs and export.
18. Organisations & Agencies operational network.
19. Communications Centre for two-way operational messaging.
20. Offline/network resilience remains cross-cutting through the existing offline queue and sync architecture.
21. Evacuation & Safe Routes with route selection, activation, recalculation, navigation and responder escort.

Important: simulated/synthetic operational values are explicitly identified in the UI where applicable. External map/navigation and emergency calling depend on the configured device/browser/provider.
